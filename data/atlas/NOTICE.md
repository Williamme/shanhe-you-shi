# Historical territory snapshots

The simplified polity outlines in this directory are derived from the
open-source **Jiangshan Atlas / 江山时序** project:

- Source: https://github.com/DeanChensj/jiangshan-map
- Copyright: DeanChensj and contributors
- License: GNU GPL v3.0

Only features whose source role is `main`, `rival`, `protectorate`, or
`neighbor` were retained. Prefecture, department, and commandery geometry from
CHGIS is deliberately excluded and is not redistributed by this project.

The source project describes its polity outlines as schematic historical
reconstructions derived in part from `aourednik/historical-basemaps` and
hand-adjusted against historical sources. They are teaching illustrations,
not surveyed borders.

The original GPL license text is available in `LICENSE-GPL-3.0.txt`.
