# Notices and third-party sources

## Historical territory snapshots

The simplified polity outlines in `data/atlas/` are adapted from:

- Jiangshan Atlas / 江山时序
- https://github.com/DeanChensj/jiangshan-map
- Copyright © 2026 DeanChensj and contributors
- License: GNU GPL v3.0

Only `main`, `rival`, `protectorate`, and `neighbor` polity features were
retained. Prefecture, department, and commandery geometry derived from CHGIS
is excluded. Those datasets are restricted to academic, non-commercial use
and are not redistributed by this project.

See `data/atlas/NOTICE.md` and `data/atlas/LICENSE-GPL-3.0.txt`.

- TailwindCSS is loaded from its CDN distribution.
- The terrain basemap uses Esri World Shaded Relief and remains subject to

## Educational content

Classical literary works are generally in the public domain. Modern
annotations, translations, summaries, routes, editorial notes, and interface
copy in this repository were organized for this educational project.

Textbook names, grade placement, and examination labels follow commonly used
editions of the national curriculum. Local editions and teaching schedules
may differ.

Video links point to third-party Bilibili pages. The videos and their content
remain the property of their respective uploaders.

## Historical accuracy

All historical borders and administrative areas are schematic teaching aids,
not surveyed boundaries. Historical dates, toponyms, and territorial claims
may differ across scholarly traditions.
